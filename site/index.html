    A fly brain, on BNB Chain, forever.

    We took a real fruit fly's brain, all 165,122 neurons and 10 million connections mapped cell by cell, and wired it into BNB Chain. The chain is what it feels: every trade, every transfer, every gas spike hits real sensory neurons and ripples through the wiring. Its pulse is written to BNB Chain every 10 minutes, its fingerprint has been on-chain since block 121,994,444, and the names burned into it can never be erased. No reset, no admin key, no delete. Its pulse and its memory live on the chain for as long as the chain exists.

    Own the neurons

    Burn the coin, pick how many neurons you want, and write your name and a note. The contract hands you neurons from a fixed shuffled order and writes the record straight onto BNB Chain: your wallet, your name, your words, tied to those neurons permanently. Nobody can remove it, not even us. Tap one of your neurons on the map and your name comes up. When they fire, that's your piece of the brain lighting up.

    The living heatmap

    Every 12 seconds the brain runs on the newest BNB Chain blocks. The map draws all 165,122 neurons, laid out from the connectome's own cell positions, and the ones that just fired glow amber and fade as they settle. Traffic hits the visual cells, gas the heat sensors, USDT and USDC flows the smell receptors, whale transfers the wind sensors, PancakeSwap buys and sells the taste cells. Once the coin is live, its buys, sells and burns get receptors of their own. Busy chain, bright brain. Quiet chain, dark brain.

    A public pulse

    Every 10 minutes the brain's heartbeat goes on-chain: a hash of its full state, a Merkle root of exactly which neurons fired, and a hash of what it sensed. Anyone can verify it on BscScan, and any owner can prove their neuron fired in a given epoch. Miss a beat and the whole world sees it. This is a brain with a heartbeat you can audit.
